# Construction brief — chair_05 (walnut organic MCM)

Studio product photos (¾ front + ¾ rear) + published dims. **Local eval only.** Bespoke-first — stick templates cannot express this.

## Character
Warm **walnut** frame with **organic tapers** and flowing lines. **Crisscrossed** under-seat structure. **Elongated rear legs** rise past the seat and cradle a **curved oval back cushion** with a clear gap above the seat (**floating back**). **Mounded** thick seat cushion, rounded plan, textured linen-blend oatmeal. Soft sculptural joins, not butt sticks.

## Members

| Member | Intent |
| --- | --- |
| Seat cushion | Thick mounded / rounded; sits on / into the underframe; fabric oatmeal. |
| Back cushion | Curved oval; same fabric; mounted to inner faces of rear uprights; **gap** above seat. |
| Front legs | Organic taper (thicker mid / toward seat, narrow at foot); soft section; black glides. |
| Rear posts | Continuous floor → above back; sculptural taper (thick mid); curve inward to follow oval back; cradle cushion sides. |
| Underframe | **Crisscrossed** wood members under seat tying the four legs — visible especially from rear ¾. Flows into legs. |
